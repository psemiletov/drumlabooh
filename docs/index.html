Features

  • DAW Compatibility
  • Linux LV2/VST3, Windows VST3 plugin format
  • Supported sample drumkit formats: Drumlabooh, Hydrogen, SFZ, and simple directories with samples
  • Drumlabooh comes with dozens of drum kits - original ones and built from samples of legendary acoustic drums and vintage drum machines. It delivers sounds for any genre - from darkwave and psytrance to jazz and rock. In addition to samples, Drumlabooh can utilize presets with "formulas" for sound synthesis. All samples are public domain and royalty-free.
  • Stereo (with built-in mixer) or multi (36 channels) output
  • Built-in mixer with Pan, Volume, mute controls and Pan mode option
  • Per-instrument FX: low-pass and high-pass filters. The Analog knob delivers that signature warm tube sound, while the Live knob (requires filters enabled) brings any samples to life, making even a bare-bones drum kit of a few one-shots sound like a real acoustic kit.
  • Up to 36 instrument slots with layers (max 127 layers or sample variants)
  • Layer change by MIDI velocity, Round Robin or Random order
  • Multi one-shot samples per slot
  • Automatic open hihat mute on hihat close
  • Kit-defined (user-defined) mute groups
  • Drumkit image (if provided) at plugin window
  • Flexible MIDI mapping modes
  • Written in C++, based on JUCE toolkit
